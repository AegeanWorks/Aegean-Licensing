You may use the marks without written permission to:

5. use a mark after your license or permission ends, except for accurate historical references.

A fork may keep the original project name only if it is clearly marked as an unofficial fork and

name and state compatibility separately.

A paid certificate does not grant trademark rights. A commercial licensee may state that it holds
an ACL-1.0 certificate and may use the standard "Aegean Licensed" badge only in the form supplied
with the certificate. Any other logo use needs written permission.

duration and a sample.
