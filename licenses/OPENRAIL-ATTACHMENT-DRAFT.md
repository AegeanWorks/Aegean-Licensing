# OpenRAIL-style Use-Restriction Attachment (Draft)

**Version 1.0-draft — October 2026**
**Status**: optional attachment; not operative unless an Order Form or a project LICENSE names it

This attachment adds use-based restrictions to Project Data or Model Weights licensed under
ASL-1.1. It is modelled on the OpenRAIL family but is not the OpenRAIL license and has not been
reviewed by counsel.

## 1. Restricted uses

You must not use the materials, or a derivative, to:

1. violate applicable law or a person's fundamental rights;
2. unlawfully surveil, identify, deanonymize or profile a person;
3. develop or deploy a weapon, or a system whose primary purpose is physical harm;
4. make a medical diagnosis, treatment or clinical decision without qualified human oversight;
5. make a legal, employment, credit, housing or immigration decision without human review and a
   lawful basis;
6. generate or distribute child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, or content
   that harasses a person;
7. facilitate fraud, phishing, malware or unauthorized access to a system; or
8. discriminate against a person or group on a protected ground.

## 2. Propagation

1. A redistributed model, weights file or dataset that contains the materials must carry this
   attachment and the ASL-1.1 notice.
2. A derivative model must not be licensed under terms that remove these restrictions.
3. A publication or model card must state that the attachment applies.

## 3. No endorsement

This attachment does not make AegeanWorks a party to your model, dataset or deployment, and does not
make it responsible for your outputs.

## 4. Precedence

If this attachment conflicts with ASL-1.1 or an Order Form, the more protective restriction applies
to the extent permitted by law. If a restriction is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.

## 5. Review

The list is a starting point for counsel and the project owner. It is not a substitute for the
OpenRAIL-M/OpenRAIL-D text when a funder, platform or regulator requires that exact license.
