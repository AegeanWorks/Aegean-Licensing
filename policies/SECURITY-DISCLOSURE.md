# Security Disclosure Policy

**Version 1.0 — October 2026**
**Contact**: `security@aegeanworks.dev` (or `aegean-licensing@proton.me` if unavailable)

## Scope

This policy covers vulnerabilities in AegeanWorks source code, released builds and the
aegean-licensing verification package. It does not cover third-party dependencies, platforms,
storefronts or a licensee's own deployment; report those to their maintainers.

## Reporting

Send a private report with:

- the affected project, version and commit;
- a description and impact;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- the configuration and platform; and
- your name and contact for credit, if you want it.

Do not open a public issue, publish details, or include personal data before coordination.

## Safe harbour

AegeanWorks will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that:

1. stays within a copy you are licensed to use;
2. avoids privacy violations, data destruction, service disruption and social engineering;
3. gives AegeanWorks a reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure; and
4. complies with applicable law.

This is not a waiver of rights against a malicious actor or a promise of immunity from third-party
claims.

## Response

AegeanWorks aims to acknowledge a report within 5 business days, give an initial assessment within
10 business days and agree a disclosure date. These are targets, not an SLA. A critical issue may
need a coordinated release; a low-impact issue may be documented without a release.

## Disclosure

The default coordinated-disclosure window is 90 days after acknowledgement. AegeanWorks may agree
a shorter or longer window when a fix, a dependency or a user-safety issue needs it. Credit is
given in the advisory unless you ask otherwise.

## No bounty

There is no paid bug-bounty programme. A report is a contribution to security, not a contract for
payment.

## Out of scope

- denial-of-service and load testing;
- spam, phishing and physical attacks;
- reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact;
- issues in a modified build or a third-party integration; and
- a missing security header with no demonstrated exploit.

When in doubt, ask first.
