# Dual-License and GPL Compatibility Policy

**Version 1.0 — October 2026**
**Contact**: `aegean-licensing@proton.me`

The Aegean personal licenses are source-available, not OSI open source, and are not compatible with
GPL-3.0 or AGPL-3.0. This policy explains the only way AegeanWorks grants a permissive or copyleft
compatible license.

## 1. No automatic dual license

Possession of a copy under AL-1.1, AGL-1.1 or ASL-1.1 does not grant a second license. A permissive
grant requires a separate written agreement signed by AegeanWorks.

## 2. Permissive grant

On request, AegeanWorks may grant a project-specific, non-exclusive license under MIT or Apache-2.0
for a named version, in writing. The grant:

- names the project, version, rights holder and scope;
- states whether it is limited to a specific downstream project;
- does not revoke the existing Aegean grant to others;
- does not grant trademark, asset, dataset or model-weight rights; and
- does not apply to a later version unless the writing says so.

## 3. GPL and AGPL projects

A project that must combine with GPL-3.0 or AGPL-3.0 needs a written permission under Section 2.
The permission should state how the combined work is licensed and which Aegean material it covers.
Without that writing, do not combine the code.

## 4. Request

Send the project, version, downstream repository, intended license, distribution model and the
reason the Aegean license is unsuitable. AegeanWorks may refuse, limit or charge for the grant. A
decision is not a waiver of any other right.

## 5. Records

A granted permission is recorded in the project's ADR and, when it covers a commercial product, in
the Order Form. It is not implied by a README badge, a fork or a public statement.

## 6. No effect on commercial terms

A permissive grant does not reduce the ACL-1.0 fees or scope for a commercial product, and does not
grant assets, data or model weights that need a separate addendum.
